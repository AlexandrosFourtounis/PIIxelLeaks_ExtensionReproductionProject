Find your place at ours

Handpicked for their heritage and location, you’ll find the best of town and country at all our places.

Each one offers all our guests something different, but always with contemporary luxury alongside colourful histories that include King Arthur and Napoleon and a warm, friendly welcome.

Once inside, you’ll find Welsh accents running through our well-stocked bars, tempting menus and individually designed bedrooms. And, whether you’re eating, drinking, staying or celebrating, you’ll be welcomed by a team who all look for ways to make your experience extra special, from our first encounter and every time you return.

We wish you a warm Welsh welcome to the Town & Country Collective!
